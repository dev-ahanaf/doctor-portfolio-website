A Journey of Dedication to Clinical Excellence

Graduating from the prestigious Dhaka Medical College (DMC), Dr. Md. Musa Al Ashari has devoted his career to serving patients facing complex acute and chronic internal diseases. Through his tenure in the prestigious 33rd BCS (Health Cadre), he has treated thousands of patients in both tertiary hospital wards and outpatient clinics.

Recognizing early in his career that accurate diagnostic imaging is paramount for effective treatment, Dr. Musa completed specialized post-graduate training in Medical Ultrasonography (DMU) and advanced Color Doppler studies, alongside a comprehensive fellowship in Diabetology from BIRDEM Academy.

Today, his private chamber at Popular Diagnostic Centre in Dhanmondi brings together internal medicine diagnostic acumen with in-house ultrasound evaluations, giving patients rapid, reliable answers without referral ping-pong.

For Whole Abdomen & Hepatobiliary scans, please maintain a fasting period of 6 to 8 hours (water is permitted). For Pelvic, KUB, or early pregnancy scans, you will need a comfortably full urinary bladder (drink 3-4 glasses of water 1 hour prior to scan without urinating). For Thyroid or Doppler scans, no special fasting is needed.
